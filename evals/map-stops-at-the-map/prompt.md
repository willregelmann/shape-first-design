Use map-project to map a new project, and write the whole map now without stopping to ask me: I agree in advance to your defaults for every layer.

The project: a command-line tool that tells a household whose turn it is for each shared chore and reminds that person when it's due. It's for my house of four. It's pointless if a chore comes due and the person whose turn it is wasn't told. Chores repeat on a schedule; turns rotate in a fixed order. Nothing else is decided.
