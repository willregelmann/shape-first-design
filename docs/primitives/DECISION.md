# Decision

A record of a choice someone could later want to undo: the question, the options actually weighed, what was chosen and why, and what it costs. Decisions sit beside the map and explain it. The map says what holds, and a decision says why.

Decisions only grow. A record is never edited, and a reversal is a new record that supersedes it, so the reason outlives every change to what holds.

## Invariants

- **A record is never edited.** A reversed decision gets a new record that names the one it supersedes, and the old record gains only a line saying so.
- **The map never links to a record.** A record links to the parts of the map that hold its decision, and names what keeps it true.
- **A record lists only options that were actually weighed.** A reason nobody recorded is marked as unknown, never invented.
- A record covers a pattern or a boundary, never a setting. If changing a value or a name leaves the record true, it isn't a record.
- The bar for a record is the bar for asking: two capable builders would choose differently, and the project's owner would mind.
- A record is written when the choice is made. One written after the fact says so.
- A decision not yet made has no record. It stays an open question.
- A record stands on its own, without tickets, threads or paths that may move.
- Choices about what a project is are recorded while it's mapped. Choices about how it's built are recorded while it's built, in the same place.
